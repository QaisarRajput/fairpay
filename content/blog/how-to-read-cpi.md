You have probably seen a headline saying inflation is up 3%. That number is useful but incomplete.

CPI is made of categories that move at different speeds.

Transportation can spike while apparel cools. Housing can remain sticky even when energy drops.

So if your spending pattern is heavy in one category, your lived inflation can diverge from headline CPI.

FairPay lets you compare your salary against both headline and category series to expose that gap.
