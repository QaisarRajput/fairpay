Suppose you earned $80,000 in June 2021.

To preserve the same buying power in June 2024, you need:

breakEven = 80000 * CPI(2024-06) / CPI(2021-06)

If that break-even value is $95,000 and your current pay is $88,000, your nominal raise did not keep up.
