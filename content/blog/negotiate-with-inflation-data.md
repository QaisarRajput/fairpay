A stronger raise conversation uses evidence, not just effort.

Start with your compensation timeline and compute your real change.

Then frame your request around purchasing power:
- Here is my nominal change over this period.
- Here is the inflation-adjusted break-even salary.
- Here is the gap in real terms.

This keeps the discussion factual and reduces ambiguity around what a competitive raise means.
