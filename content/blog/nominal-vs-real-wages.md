Your salary can rise in dollar terms while your purchasing power falls.

Nominal pay is the number on your paycheck. Real pay adjusts that number by inflation.

If your costs rise faster than your wage, your raise is not a real raise.

That is exactly why FairPay compares your salary change against CPI series over the same period.

The result is simple:
- Positive real change means you gained buying power.
- Zero means you stayed flat.
- Negative means your raise was a real cut.
